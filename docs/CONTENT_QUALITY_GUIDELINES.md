# Boilabin Content Quality Guidelines

## Goal

Boilabin copy should sound factual, useful, and specific. It should not sound like generic AI marketing or an unsupported marketplace claim.

## Core Rules

- Use honest factual copy.
- Avoid bragging without proof.
- Explain what the page actually offers.
- Use product/category-specific language.
- Write for humans first.
- Support search and AI understanding with facts, not hype.
- Avoid trust, luxury, top-choice, or market-leader claims without evidence.
- Avoid scammy marketplace language.
- Keep Bangladesh context concrete: delivery area, payment method, stock, returns, contact, and product type.
- Treat Search Everywhere copy as factual page explanation, not keyword stuffing.
- If a business fact is not confirmed, omit it or say where the buyer can check it.

## Avoid These Patterns

- unsupported national trust or luxury-positioning slogans
- global-superiority shopping slogans
- destination-style superlatives
- all-in-one shopping slogans
- confidence slogans without evidence
- vague discovery slogans
- origin or condition guarantees without proof
- price-superiority claims unless price evidence exists
- popularity claims unless the number is true and documented

## Better Copy Pattern

Use:

```text
Who this page is for + what products are here + where it serves + practical buying detail.
```

## Examples

Bad:

```text
Bangladesh-focused shopping with products across multiple categories.
```

Better:

```text
Boilabin is a Bangladesh-based online marketplace for products across toys, electronics, beauty, lifestyle, and collectibles.
```

Bad:

```text
Shop a top-tier Hot Wheels collection.
```

Better:

```text
Browse Hot Wheels cars, multipacks, and collectible die-cast models available from sellers in Bangladesh.
```

Bad:

```text
Discover new products curated for you.
```

Better:

```text
Browse new electronics, toys, beauty products, and home items added to the Boilabin catalog.
```

## Product Page Guidance

Good product copy should include:

- exact product name;
- brand if known;
- category;
- size/model/variant details;
- compatibility where relevant;
- what is included;
- price and availability if accurate;
- delivery/return note if accurate;
- Bangladesh-specific buying context if useful.

Avoid:

- generic luxury-quality claims;
- invented authenticity claims;
- fake urgency;
- exaggerated discounts;
- vague "best seller" claims unless the product is actually marked that way.

Admin product descriptions should encourage facts that can be checked:

- material, size, color, model, and compatibility;
- included items and variant differences;
- condition notes where relevant;
- warranty, return, or delivery notes only when the policy is known;
- image alt text that names the actual product.

Admin product descriptions should not encourage price-superiority, delivery-speed, checkout-safety, luxury-quality, or origin-verification claims unless those claims are backed by approved policy.

## Category Page Guidance

Good category copy should include:

- what product types are in the category;
- common brands or subcategories if accurate;
- buyer intent in Bangladesh;
- delivery/payment facts;
- filters buyers can use.

## Search Everywhere Guidance

AI answer systems and search engines need clear facts:

- who Boilabin is;
- what Boilabin sells;
- where it operates;
- how payments and returns work;
- which product pages are indexable;
- structured data that matches visible page content.

Do not write only for keywords. Search systems can understand factual structure better than empty claims.

For Boilabin, Search Everywhere Optimization means factual crawlable content plus structured data that matches visible page facts. It does not mean repeating superiority, trust, or luxury-positioning phrases.

## Metadata Guidance

Metadata should summarize what the page actually contains:

- product pages: product name, price, category, availability, and visible details;
- category pages: category name, visible product count, prices/images availability, and subcategory context;
- support pages: the policy or task explained on the page;
- social previews: the same factual summary used on the page.

Avoid adding delivery speed, payment availability, seller status, authenticity, review counts, or warranty details unless that same fact is visible and current on the page.

## Structured Data And Social Preview Guidance

Structured data is page evidence for search systems, not a place to add sales claims. Before adding Product, Offer, shipping, return, payment, review, Organization, FAQ, or ItemList fields, check that the same fact is visible on the page or supplied by current product data.

Keep schema conservative:

- do not add GTIN, MPN, brand, rating, review, shipping time, return method, payment provider, or seller-marketplace fields without real source data;
- do not use schema to claim authenticity, trust, speed, guarantees, or broad coverage;
- keep Cash on Delivery wording factual and do not imply online gateways are live unless checkout supports them;
- keep Open Graph image copy short, factual, and consistent with visible site facts.

## Review Checklist Before Publishing Copy

- Is every claim provable?
- Would a buyer understand what is actually available?
- Does the text mention the specific product/category?
- Is the language calm and useful?
- Does it avoid trust, luxury, top-choice, market-leader, and superlative claims unless proven?
- Does it help a search engine or AI answer system identify the page topic?
