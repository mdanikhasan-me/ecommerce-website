# Boilabin Content Quality Guidelines

## Goal

Boilabin copy should sound factual, useful, and specific. It should not sound like generic AI marketing or an unsupported marketplace claim.

## Core Rules

- Use honest factual copy.
- Avoid bragging without proof.
- Explain what the page actually offers.
- Use product/category-specific language.
- Write for humans first.
- Support search and AI understanding with facts, not hype.
- Never claim "trusted", "premium", "best", or "leading" without evidence.
- Avoid scammy marketplace language.
- Keep Bangladesh context concrete: delivery area, payment method, stock, returns, contact, and product type.

## Avoid These Patterns

- "Bangladesh's most trusted premium marketplace"
- "world-class shopping"
- "ultimate destination"
- "one-stop shop"
- "shop with confidence"
- "discover amazing products"
- "authentic guaranteed"
- "best price" unless price evidence exists
- "loved by thousands" unless the number is true and documented

## Better Copy Pattern

Use:

```text
Who this page is for + what products are here + where it serves + practical buying detail.
```

## Examples

Bad:

```text
Bangladesh's most trusted premium marketplace.
```

Better:

```text
Boilabin is a Bangladesh-based online marketplace for products across toys, electronics, beauty, lifestyle, and collectibles.
```

Bad:

```text
Shop the best premium Hot Wheels collection.
```

Better:

```text
Browse Hot Wheels cars, multipacks, and collectible die-cast models available from sellers in Bangladesh.
```

Bad:

```text
Discover amazing products curated just for you.
```

Better:

```text
Browse new electronics, toys, beauty products, and home items added to the Boilabin catalog.
```

## Product Page Guidance

Good product copy should include:

- exact product name;
- brand if known;
- category;
- size/model/variant details;
- compatibility where relevant;
- what is included;
- price and availability if accurate;
- delivery/return note if accurate;
- Bangladesh-specific buying context if useful.

Avoid:

- generic "premium quality";
- invented authenticity claims;
- fake urgency;
- exaggerated discounts;
- vague "best seller" claims unless the product is actually marked that way.

## Category Page Guidance

Good category copy should include:

- what product types are in the category;
- common brands or subcategories if accurate;
- buyer intent in Bangladesh;
- delivery/payment facts;
- filters buyers can use.

## Search Everywhere Guidance

AI answer systems and search engines need clear facts:

- who Boilabin is;
- what Boilabin sells;
- where it operates;
- how payments and returns work;
- which product pages are indexable;
- structured data that matches visible page content.

Do not write only for keywords. Search systems can understand factual structure better than empty claims.

## Review Checklist Before Publishing Copy

- Is every claim provable?
- Would a buyer understand what is actually available?
- Does the text mention the specific product/category?
- Is the language calm and useful?
- Does it avoid "trusted", "premium", "best", "leading", and "ultimate" unless proven?
- Does it help a search engine or AI answer system identify the page topic?
