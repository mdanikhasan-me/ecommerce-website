# Step 226 - Image Search And Product Image Readiness

## Scope

Reviewed readiness for Google Images, Lens-style discovery, and product image quality.

## Current Strengths

- Product/category image source-of-truth repairs were completed in earlier steps.
- Media upload policy exists.
- WebP-preferred upload processing and limits exist.
- Product/category image SEO is covered in docs.

## Current Gaps

- Derived image variants are not implemented.
- Object storage/CDN is not selected.
- Merchant feed image requirements are not implemented.
- Product image quality and alt text need browser/human QA at scale.
- Public hosted image URLs do not exist yet.

## Future Evidence Needed

- Hosted product image URLs render anonymously.
- Product images are stable and match product content.
- Category images match categories.
- Images are not blocked by auth/robots.
- Large catalog media strategy is approved before seller/vendor scale.
