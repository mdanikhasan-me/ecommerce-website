Read-only finding: after Steps 366 and 367, safe callback target checks blocked auth/API/framework/static/metadata routes by pathname, but encoded pathnames such as /%61uth/login, /%61pi/orders, /%72obots.txt, or /%2Fexample.com/phish could bypass the blocklist because validation used the raw encoded pathname.
Resolution: safe callback normalization now decodes the URL pathname for validation, rejects malformed encodings and decoded protocol-relative/backslash shapes, and applies the existing target blocklists to the decoded pathname while preserving safe encoded internal callbacks.
